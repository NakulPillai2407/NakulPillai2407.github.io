# About

I'm Nakul Pillai, an MSc Financial Technology student at Imperial College London, with a BSc in Economics and Data Science from the University of Southampton.

Holdout Research is my personal research project: a place to develop trading strategy ideas with a consistent process and to document them honestly. The name comes from the holdout holdout, the data a strategy never sees while it is being designed, which is where every idea gets judged. It is not a company or a live trading operation.
